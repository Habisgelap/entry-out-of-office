## Winner: #2

> Gone until Monday. My inbox is being handled by a man named Greg who does not work here.

**Why it won:** the twist lands late and reframes the whole reply — a named stranger
("Greg") who "does not work here" is the most specific image of the ten, and it is
exactly the kind of thing a real person would set as a joke OOO. It scores top on
surprise (the reader expects a return date, gets an unauthorised deputy) and
specificity (a named person, a concrete absurdity), while staying plausible as an
auto-reply and using every word — nothing can be cut without losing the laugh.
Runner-up #6 (houseplant promoted twice) has a good twist but reads less like a
reply a real person would actually send; #10 is plausible but its twist is telegraphed.
